# TrustRx Controlled Pilot Protocol

## Aim

Test whether TrustRx can reduce time from a frontline syndromic observation to authorised reviewer notification while preserving data quality, privacy and integrity evidence in one bounded deployment.

## Scope

- One state/implementing partner.
- One LGA or a comparably small facility network.
- One partner-approved syndromic or priority-disease reporting workflow.
- Designated reporters and reviewers only.
- Sixty-day operational period after training and acceptance testing.

## Preconditions

- Written partner governance/permission and named response owner.
- Current-state workflow and baseline latency documented through discovery interviews.
- Approved minimum data set, thresholds, escalation RACI, retention schedule and external-handoff route.
- DPIA, DPA, training material, support hours and incident contacts completed.
- No claim of SORMAS/DHIS2 integration without a tested and approved connection or handoff procedure.

## Measures

| Outcome | Measure |
|---|---|
| Timeliness | signal receipt to reviewer notification; reviewer action compared to current baseline |
| Reporting quality | completeness, duplicate rate, correction/follow-up rate and message failure rate |
| Integrity | percentage of eligible accepted events with a valid HCS receipt and successful private-event verification |
| Usability | trained/active reporter rate and short structured feedback |
| Safety | privacy/security/clinical workflow incidents and resolution time |
| Interoperability | partner-confirmed official handoffs and acknowledgement status |

## Stop conditions

Suspend the pilot for an unresolved high-severity privacy/security event, unauthorised processing, inability to maintain reviewer coverage, a material workflow safety failure or partner instruction.
