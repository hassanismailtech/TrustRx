Privacy information

This public site is not a health-data channel.

The current site has no TrustRx account, reporting form or health-data intake. Do not submit patient information, health reports, credentials or urgent requests through this website or public repository.

Product privacy direction

The future operational product is designed around minimum-necessary data, private tenant-scoped storage, human access controls and non-sensitive integrity commitments. Any real-data pilot requires dedicated privacy and legal review.

This page describes the current public-site boundary; it is not a completed legal privacy notice for a live health-data service. Formal notices, processor disclosures and data-subject processes require the final hosting, contact and pilot model.
