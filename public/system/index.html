System

From the first field signal to an accountable decision.

TrustRx is designed around a protected operational flow—not a public dashboard or consumer reporting tool.

01LiteRegistered reporter submits a strict structured signal by SMS or USSD.
02CorePrivate event, audit trail, idempotency and integrity work are recorded together.
03ConsoleAuthorised reviewer sees a bounded queue and records a human decision.
04VerifyNon-sensitive proof can establish sequence and provenance without revealing the record.
05BridgeVerified events are queued for an approved partner-specific handoff.
TrustRx does not replace IDSR, SORMAS or DHIS2. Any integration requires partner-approved mapping and sandbox verification.
