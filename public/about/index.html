About TrustRx

Public-health infrastructure deserves product discipline.

TrustRx is being built as a distinct, pre-pilot startup focused on the final mile of authorised epidemic-intelligence workflows in low-connectivity settings.

What we are building

A private, accountable workflow from structured field signal to authorised reviewer to approved official-system handoff—with integrity evidence that does not expose the underlying record.

How we build

We work from real workflow validation, data minimisation, human oversight, tested engineering controls and explicit non-claims. A visual prototype is not a live health service.
