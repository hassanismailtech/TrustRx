One deliberate route from the field to authorised response.

01ReportAuthorised field worker uses structured SMS or USSD.
02ProtectTrustRx validates and records the minimum necessary event privately.
03ProveA non-sensitive integrity commitment establishes event provenance.
04ReviewAn authorised surveillance reviewer evaluates the signal and evidence.
05HandoffAn approved workflow routes verified signals into the official system.
Trust, made operational

Built to strengthen the last mile—not replace the system.

01 / ACCESS

Offline first

Structured reporting works for low-connectivity environments. The product begins with field reality, not a web form.

02 / PRIVACY

Private by design

Operational records stay in a controlled private store. Public-verification mechanisms never carry raw report content.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Human governed

TrustRx supports review and evidence. It does not diagnose, confirm outbreaks, or trigger public action autonomously.
