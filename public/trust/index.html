Trust boundary

Evidence without exposure.

TrustRx separates protected operational records from integrity proof, and separates receipt from verification and action.

What TrustRx does

  • Supports structured reporting in low-connectivity contexts.
  • Keeps operational records private and tenant-scoped.
  • Records reviewer decisions and approved handoff intent.
  • Creates non-sensitive integrity commitments.

What TrustRx does not do

  • Diagnose, prescribe, confirm outbreaks or issue public alerts.
  • Publish raw health records, patient data, facilities or reporters.
  • Replace official surveillance systems.
  • Claim a deployed partner integration without evidence.
