Pilot pathway

A disciplined path to learning, not a shortcut to live deployment.

Pilot conversations begin with one defined reporting workflow, an accountable owner and controlled synthetic rehearsal.

Before any pilot

  • Workflow-owner discovery and field usability validation.
  • Privacy, security, safety and data-processing review.
  • Approved reporter/reviewer roles and manual fallback SOP.
  • Synthetic end-to-end rehearsal and restore evidence.

What to bring to a briefing

  • A defined facility/programme scope.
  • Existing reporting and escalation workflow.
  • Authorised operational and privacy owners.
  • Constraints around connectivity, language and integration.
Do not send health data, patient information, urgent incident reports or credentials through this site. A controlled contact route will be added after communications and privacy review.
